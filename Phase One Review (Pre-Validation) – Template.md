## 1. Purpose of this Review

This Phase One review assesses whether the material available for \[model name\] is sufficient for Model Risk Management (MRM) to carry out an independent validation in line with PRA SS1/23 and the Bank's \[insert policy name\].

SS1/23 sets clear expectations on the evidence a validation relies on:

- **Documentation (Principle 3.5):** model documentation should be detailed enough for an independent third party with relevant expertise to understand how the model works, identify its key assumptions and limitations, and replicate its results.
- **Owner accountability (Principle 2.4):** model owners are accountable for providing all the information needed for validation to take place.
- **Scope of review (Principle 4.2):** an independent review should cover all model components, including inputs, calculations and outputs, and critically analyse the quality and extent of development evidence.
- **DQMs (Principle 1.1):** deterministic quantitative methods not classified as models should be subject to sound and clearly documented management controls.

This review is not a validation. It does not assess the conceptual soundness, performance or appropriateness of the \[model / DQM\], and it does not express an opinion on its fitness for use. It records:

- The material MRM received and reviewed (Section 2).
- MRM's current understanding of the \[model / DQM\], based on that material (Section 3).
- Where the available evidence falls short of what an independent validation requires (Section 4).
- The information MRM requests from the model owner to proceed (Section 5).

## Appendix: About Independent Validation

An independent validation is a review by MRM, which is independent of the model's development and use, to confirm that a model is conceptually sound, works as intended, and is used appropriately. PRA SS1/23 expects banks to validate their models independently and to manage model risk through documentation, testing, monitoring and governance.

To do this, MRM needs evidence it can review and, where relevant, reproduce. Material held only as knowledge within the team, or in files without explanation, cannot be relied on for an independent conclusion. This is why the documentation requested in Section 5 is needed before the validation can start.

Source: [PRA SS1/23 – Model risk management principles for banks](https://prarulebook.co.uk/guidance/supervisory-statements/ss01-23--model-risk-management-principles-for-banks/4---model-risk-management-principles-for--banks/17-05-2024?p=1), effective 17 May 2024.
