# End of Year Performance Review 2026 – Self-Assessment

Nov 5, 2026 · @Ariel Gimenez

## A. Achievement against goals

**1. Mentoring and leadership – 65%**

I supported new joiners on five reviews: the ICAAP Stress Testing DQM, the Capital Management DQM, and the Annual Reviews of the NMD model, the Stress NII model and the Stress Credit Loss module. I also gave detailed quality reviews of colleagues' reports before they went to the Head of MRM. This gave me valuable experience in guiding others and improved the quality of team output. I recognise there are aspects of mentoring and leadership I still need to develop, and I will keep building on this next year.

**2. Communication and professional presence – 70%**

My visibility and ownership increased this year. I acted as MRM's link with HWAM and as the main contact with Internal Audit, which led to the closure of the audit findings. I also continued as MOC Secretary, organising meetings, preparing decks and writing minutes. Together these gave me more exposure in meetings and a more active role with stakeholders. I aim to continue strengthening my presence in senior forums.

**3. Regulatory and balance-sheet risk knowledge – 70%**

I gained broad practical knowledge through the reviews I completed: the Climate Scenario model, the ILAAP Stress Testing DQM, the Hometrack AVM, the Strategic Asset Allocation model and the IFRS 9 Annual Review. Writing the new IRB and non-IRB Validation Instructions also deepened my regulatory understanding. However, I still need to build stronger technical knowledge in some areas, particularly IFRS 9, where my understanding remains limited.

**4. ML and AI technical fluency – 30%**

Progress on this goal was limited, as competing priorities left little time for structured learning. Most of my development came in recent months, while helping to write the AI MRM framework and starting research on AI risks and validation approaches. My knowledge is still at a foundational level, and this will be a key focus next year.

**5. HWAM model identification and oversight – 50%**

I engaged Wealth Management stakeholders to start onboarding their models and embed MRM requirements in their frameworks. I also completed the Strategic Asset Allocation model validation and began scoping Voyant. Progress was slower than planned because of delayed responses from the business and other priorities that arose during the year. I also contributed to model inventory maintenance and the vendor tool search, and I am now helping colleagues write the technical specifications for the current inventory.

**Changes in priorities / support needed**

Unplanned work affected delivery against Goals 4 and 5, mainly the two new Validation Instructions, audit closure, inventory work and mentoring. Next year I would benefit from dedicated time and formal training in AI/ML and IFRS 9. Clearer escalation routes with HWAM would also help secure timely engagement from model owners.

## D. Summary

2026 has been a year of broad delivery and growing responsibility. I completed five validations and reviews across climate, liquidity, collateral valuation, asset allocation and IFRS 9 models. Beyond validation, I wrote two new Validation Instructions, closed the Internal Audit findings and continued as MOC Secretary. My role also widened: I mentored new joiners across five reviews, quality-reviewed colleagues' work and acted as MRM's link with HWAM, which increased my visibility and ownership within the function.
